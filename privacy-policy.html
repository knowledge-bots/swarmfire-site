SwarmFire: Hold the Line — Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

SwarmFire: Hold the Line (“the game”) is made by Knowledge Bots. This policy covers the game on Android and iOS.

The short version

The game collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it doesn’t connect to the internet. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels cleared, stars, best times and scores, Cores and Armory upgrades, the daily run streak) and your settings (sound, music, haptics) are saved in the game’s own storage on your device. They never leave it. Deleting the game deletes them.

Sharing

When you tap SHARE on the end screen, the game hands a line of text (for example, the wave you reached) to your device’s share sheet, or copies it to your clipboard. You choose whether and where to send it. The game doesn’t see where it goes.

Permissions

The game uses your device’s vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in its settings. It asks for no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version ever needs to collect anything (for example, if a paid unlock is added, purchases would be handled by Google Play or the App Store), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
